Reporting something

Every post, comment, chat message, and user can be reported from inside the app — the ••• menu on a post or a conversation, or the Report button on a profile. Reports reach a moderation queue that a person reviews, and we aim to act within 24 hours.

If you cannot reach the in-app report flow, or you need to reach us about something urgent, email nalgosupport@gmail.com.

Blocking someone

You can block any user from their profile or from your conversation with them. A block is mutual and immediate: neither of you sees the other's posts, comments, or messages, and neither can send the other a direct message.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Account Settings → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and removes your profile, posts, comments, and messages from our servers.
