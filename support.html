Support

Help with the Nalgo app.

Email us at nalgosupport@gmail.com. A person reads every message.

Nalgo is run by Ethan Toushek and Thomas Gaudet. We are a small team, and email is the way to reach us. If you are reporting a problem, it helps to include:

  • your Nalgo username
  • your device and iOS version
  • what you were doing when it happened
  • a screenshot, if you have one

Reporting a post, message, or user

Every post, comment, chat message, and user can be reported from inside the app — the ••• menu on a post or a conversation, or the Report button on a profile. Reports reach a moderation queue that a person reviews, and we aim to act within 24 hours.

If something is urgent or you cannot reach the report flow, email nalgosupport@gmail.com.

Blocking someone

Block any user from their profile or from your conversation with them. A block is mutual and immediate: neither of you sees the other's posts, comments, or messages, and neither can message the other.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Account Settings → Delete account. Deletion is permanent and takes effect right away. If you cannot sign in, see Delete Your Account for how to ask us to delete it by email.

Who can use Nalgo

You must be at least 17 years old. See the Terms & Community Guidelines for the full rules.

Privacy

What the app collects and how it is used is in the Privacy Policy. Privacy questions go to the same address: nalgosupport@gmail.com.
