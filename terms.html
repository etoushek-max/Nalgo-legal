4. Community Guidelines — zero tolerance for abuse

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users on Nalgo. Do not post, send, or share:

  • Harassment or bullying — targeting a person, pile-ons, unwanted repeated contact.
  • Hate speech — attacks on people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, disability, or serious disease.
  • Threats or incitement of violence, or glorification of it.
  • Sexual content involving minors — reported to the authorities, always, with no exception.
  • Non-consensual sexual content, including intimate images shared without consent.
  • Pornography and graphic sexual content.
  • Promotion of self-harm or suicide.
  • Illegal goods and services — drugs, weapons, stolen data, fraud.
  • Spam, scams, phishing, or manipulation of feeds, votes, or reactions.
  • Impersonation of another person or organisation.
  • Doxxing — publishing someone's private information without their consent.
  • Infringing content — anything that breaches someone else's copyright or trademark.

5. Reporting and enforcement

Every post, comment, chat message, and user can be reported from inside the app. Reports go to a moderation queue reviewed by our team, and we aim to act on them within 24 hours.

Some content is refused before it is ever published: the app screens messages for sexual content involving minors and for slurs, and will not send them.

When something breaks these guidelines we may, depending on severity and history:

  • remove the content;
  • issue a cool-off period, during which the account can read but not post;
  • permanently suspend the account, which also hides everything it has written.

Severe violations — child sexual abuse material, credible threats of violence — skip the ladder and result in immediate permanent suspension, and are referred to the relevant authorities.

If you believe we got it wrong, you can appeal from inside the app and a person will look at it again.

6. Blocking

You can block any user. A block is mutual and immediate: neither of you sees the other's posts, comments, or messages, and neither can send the other a direct message.

7. Notifications

Nalgo can send push notifications to your device for direct messages and for friend requests. They are off until you turn them on in Settings, where you can also turn either type off on its own, and you can switch them off entirely in your device settings at any time.

A notification may show who contacted you and the opening of their message, so it can appear on your lock screen. Delivery depends on Apple and Google, who operate the push services your device is connected to, and on your network — we cannot promise a notification will arrive, or arrive promptly. Do not rely on them for anything time-critical.
