5. Reporting and enforcement

Every post, comment, chat message, and user can be reported from inside the app. Reports go to a moderation queue reviewed by our team, and we aim to act on them within 24 hours.

Some content is refused before it is ever published: the app screens messages for sexual content involving minors and for slurs, and will not send them.

When something breaks these guidelines we may, depending on severity and history:

  • remove the content;
  • issue a cool-off period, during which the account can read but not post;
  • permanently suspend the account, which also hides everything it has written.

Severe violations — child sexual abuse material, credible threats of violence — skip the ladder and result in immediate permanent suspension, and are referred to the relevant authorities.

If you believe we got it wrong, you can appeal from inside the app and a person will look at it again.
